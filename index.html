ABOUT THE AUCTION

You won't find another pretentious collection of incredibly expensive antiques here. Our lots were never supposed to end up at an auction at all. After all, who needs a teenage girl's diary or a painting that even a child could draw? Who needs all these strange things created by amateurs?

Too complicated or, on the contrary, too simple. Ahead of their time. Torn apart by critics. Rejected and misunderstood. Once, they seemed like failures. But despite criticism, apparent simplicity, or complexity, today it is impossible to imagine modern culture without these works. When no one believed in them, their creators kept believing. And now these lots have become iconic.
